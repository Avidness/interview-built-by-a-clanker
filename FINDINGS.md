## Checked and found clean
- `PUT /cart/:itemId` ownership check and quantity validation.
- Money rounding (rounded once on the total, not per line).
- Fastify hook scoping: `/personas` and `/auth/*` are not gated by the cart/favorites/checkout hooks.
- With `ENFORCE_AUTH=true`, a missing token yields 401 and the handler does not run.
- Sort comparators (numeric, `localeCompare` for names); `maxPrice`, `specialty` and `tier` filters.
- Cart and checkout total arithmetic on the server.
- `SearchBar` debounce and `updateSearch` URL cleanup.
